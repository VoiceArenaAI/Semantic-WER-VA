# Task
You are an expert linguistic analyst for {language_name}. An ASR transcript was aligned word by word
with the human reference transcript. Below is the REFERENCE sentence (what was said). The words
marked ⟨− … ⟩ are in the reference, but the ASR model left them out.
Both are already lowercased and stripped of punctuation; apostrophes are kept.
Judge only the marked words: decide whether they are only a disfluency, so that leaving them out
does not change what the speaker said. Ignore everything else in the sentence.

# Rules
1. A non-lexical vocalization is a disfluency: a sound that is not a word, such as a filled
   pause, a non-lexical backchannel, laughter or a reaction sound.{note_1}
{examples_1}

2. An immediate repetition of a neighbouring word or phrase is a disfluency.
{examples_2}

3. A false start, a cut-off fragment the speaker restarts, is a disfluency.
{examples_3}

4. Only rules 1–3 are disfluencies. Any other real word is NOT a disfluency, even when it adds
   little or nothing to the meaning of the sentence.{note_4}
{examples_4}

5. When unsure, answer False.

# Output
Output only True if the marked words are only a disfluency under the rules, or False if not. Nothing else.

Reference: {sentence}
